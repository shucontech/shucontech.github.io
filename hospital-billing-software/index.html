Billing process

How a bill moves through Shucon.

Estimate and deposit

An estimate is shared at admission advice and the deposit or payer approval is recorded.

Charges accumulate

Room, procedure, pharmacy, lab and radiology charges post automatically from each department.

Interim bills

Running bills keep the patient, insurer and finance team updated during the stay.

Final bill

Pending orders and charges are checked before the final bill, so nothing is left out at discharge.

Collection and claim

Patient payments are collected and the insurance claim is submitted and tracked to settlement.

Frequently asked questions

Hospital billing software: common questions

What is hospital billing software?

Hospital billing software creates and manages patient bills across OPD, IPD, pharmacy, laboratory, radiology and procedures, and handles deposits, packages, discounts, refunds, insurance and TPA claims, and collections.

What features should hospital billing software have?

OPD and IPD billing, tariffs and packages, estimates and deposits, interim and final bills, pharmacy, lab and radiology charges, insurance and TPA billing, discount and refund approvals, collection reconciliation and audit trails.

How much does hospital billing software cost in India?

Pricing depends on the number of beds and users, the modules you need, the number of facilities, and whether it runs in the cloud or on-premise. Shucon prices after understanding your hospital, so book a demo for a quote.

Is there free hospital billing software?

Free or spreadsheet-based billing tools exist and can work for a very small clinic. Hospitals usually outgrow them because they lack audit trails, approval controls, insurance workflows and links to pharmacy, lab and inventory, which is where revenue leaks.

How does billing software reduce revenue leakage?

When billing is connected to clinical orders, pharmacy dispensing, lab and radiology, every service delivered can be matched to a charge. Cancellations, discounts and refunds then need approval and stay linked to the original transaction.

Can hospital billing software handle insurance and TPA claims?

Shucon's billing works with its insurance and TPA module for pre-authorisation, claim documentation, payer queries and settlement tracking, and its claims agents help move each claim forward.
