Frequently asked questions

AI voice agents for hospitals: common questions

What is an AI voice agent for hospitals?

It is an AI agent that answers and makes patient phone calls in natural language. It can handle enquiries, book or reschedule appointments, share preparation instructions, follow up after discharge and collect feedback, and it hands over to staff whenever a human is needed.

Can it handle both incoming and outgoing calls?

Yes. Incoming agents answer enquiries and bookings around the clock, while outgoing agents run reminders, admission and surgery follow-ups, missed-appointment recovery, payment reminders and patient satisfaction calls.

How does it improve OPD to IPD conversion?

Patients advised admission or surgery often drop off without a follow-up. The agent calls them back, answers common questions about cost, insurance and preparation, and books the next step or routes the patient to a counsellor, so fewer advised admissions are lost.

Which languages does the voice agent support?

Agents can be configured for English, Hindi and other regional languages, so patients can speak in the language they are most comfortable with.

What happens when a patient needs a human?

The agent follows defined escalation rules. Clinical concerns, complaints or complex requests are transferred to the right desk with the call context, so the patient does not have to repeat themselves.
