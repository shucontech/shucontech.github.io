Frequently asked questions

Revenue leakage detection agents: common questions

What is hospital revenue leakage?

Revenue leakage is income a hospital has earned but never collects. Common causes are unbilled services and consumables, pharmacy dispensing that does not match billing, cancelled bills that are not reconnected, incorrect tariffs or discounts, and claim deductions.

How do AI agents detect revenue leakage?

The agents reconcile clinical orders, pharmacy dispensing, inventory movement, billing, payments and claims continuously. When a record does not match its expected trail, such as a dispensed drug with no bill line, the agent raises an exception with the evidence attached.

What happens after leakage is detected?

Each exception is routed to the person accountable for it, with a recommended action and a deadline. Agents follow up until the item is resolved, so detection turns into recovered revenue rather than another report.

Which departments does it cover?

Billing, pharmacy, inventory, OT and procedure charges, bed and package billing, cancellations and refunds, discounts, payments and insurance claims. Hospitals can start with one area and expand.

Does it work with our current hospital software?

Yes. Revenue leakage agents connect to your existing HMS, pharmacy and billing data. No rip-and-replace is required.
