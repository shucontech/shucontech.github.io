Frequently asked questions

Insurance claim AI agents: common questions

What do insurance claim AI agents do for a hospital?

They watch every insured admission from pre-authorization to settlement. The agents check documentation readiness, flag missing reports or signatures, track TPA and insurer queries, remind the responsible team member and escalate claims that are at risk of delay, deduction or denial.

Do they work with TPAs, private insurers and government schemes?

Yes. Workflows can be configured for TPA-administered policies, direct insurer claims, corporate tie-ups and government health schemes such as PM-JAY and CGHS, each with its own document checklist, approval steps and follow-up rules.

How do the agents help reduce claim deductions and denials?

Most deductions come from preventable gaps: missing documents, unbilled or mismatched line items, tariff differences and late responses to queries. The agents compare clinical, billing and claim records continuously and route each gap to an owner before the file is submitted.

Do the agents submit claims automatically?

Submission and other consequential steps can stay human-approved. Agents prepare, check and prioritise the work, while your insurance desk keeps control of what is sent to payers.

Do we need to replace our HMS to use them?

No. Claim agents can run over your existing hospital management system and billing data, or natively on the Shucon HMS if you choose to move to it later.
