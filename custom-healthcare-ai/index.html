Frequently asked questions

Custom healthcare AI agents: common questions

What kinds of custom AI agents can you build?

Agents for any repeatable healthcare workflow: document processing, approvals, compliance checks, referral management, procurement, quality audits, internal reporting and patient or partner communication.

How long does it take to build a custom agent?

Most projects start with one well-defined workflow. Discovery maps your SOP, systems and decisions first, then the agent is built, tested with your team and expanded in phases.

Will the agent follow our SOPs and approval rules?

Yes. Custom agents are built around your SOPs, permissions and escalation paths, and can require human approval for any consequential action.

Can custom agents connect to our existing software?

Yes. Agents can work with your HMS, spreadsheets, email, WhatsApp, documents and other applications your teams already use.

How is patient data kept secure?

Agents run with role-based access, audit trails and deployment options including on-premise, so sensitive data can stay within your environment.
