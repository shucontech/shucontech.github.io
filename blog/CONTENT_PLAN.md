## Reusable article requirements

1. Unique title, description, canonical, Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
2. One H1; logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
3. Visible author, publication date and reading time.
4. `Article` or `BlogPosting` JSON-LD and `BreadcrumbList`.
5. `FAQPage` only when the same questions and answers are visible on the page.
6. At least one contextual link to a commercial route and two relevant internal resources.
7. CTA ladder where appropriate: calculator → checklist → Hospital Revenue Review.
8. No unsupported benchmarks, customer identities, certifications or location claims.
9. Add every published article to the blog hub and XML sitemap.
