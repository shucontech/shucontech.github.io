Frequently asked questions

Hospital management software: common questions

What modules does the Shucon hospital management system include?

OPD, IPD, EMR, front desk and appointments, billing, pharmacy, inventory and purchase, laboratory, radiology, OT, insurance and TPA, MIS and analytics, all on one connected patient and transaction record.

How is an AI hospital management system different from a traditional HMS?

A traditional HMS records transactions. Shucon’s HMS is built for AI agents that monitor those transactions, detect leakage and exceptions, and tell each team what needs to happen next.

Is it suitable for small and mid-size hospitals?

Yes. The platform is designed for multi-specialty hospitals, nursing homes and hospital groups, and can be deployed module by module.

Can it be deployed on-premise or on the cloud?

Both. Hospitals can choose cloud or on-premise deployment based on their infrastructure, connectivity and data residency requirements.

Can we migrate from our existing HMS?

Yes. Migration can be phased by department, and Shucon agents can run over your existing system while the move happens.
