What we do for you

From your first enquiry to your post-discharge follow-up, our care managers handle every step so you can focus on getting better.

Top Hospital Network

Access to JCI- and NABH-accredited hospitals across Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad and Dehradun — matched to your condition and budget.

Second Opinion Video OPD

Get a written, expert second opinion from a senior Indian specialist over a secure video OPD — usually within 48 hours of submitting your reports.

End-to-End Care Coordination

A dedicated care manager handles everything — appointments, admission, payments, language support and discharge — from arrival to your safe return home.

Transparent Cost Estimates

All-inclusive treatment estimates with no hidden charges — covering surgery, hospital stay, medications and recovery, in your preferred currency.

Visa & Travel Assistance

Medical visa invitation letters, airport pickup, hotel bookings near the hospital, attendant visa support and on-ground translators.

Post-treatment Follow-up

Regular video OPD follow-ups with your treating doctor for up to 12 months after discharge, with shared digital records and prescriptions.
