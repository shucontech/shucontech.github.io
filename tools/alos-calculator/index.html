How this ALOS calculator works

Average Length of Stay divides all the inpatient days used in a period by the number of patients who left hospital in that period, whether discharged or expired. It shows how long, on average, each admission kept a bed.

ALOS = Total inpatient days in the period ÷ Total discharges (including deaths) in the period

Use inpatient days for admitted patients only, normally the sum of the daily midnight census. Do not mix in OPD observation hours unless your hospital has a formal short-stay unit that it reports separately. Use the same period for inpatient days and discharges, and the same definitions every month, so ALOS can be compared over time and across branches.

ALOS calculation formula: worked example

A hospital records 2,400 inpatient days and 600 discharges (including deaths) in October, a 31-day month.

StepCalculationResult
ALOS2,400 ÷ 6004.0 days
After better discharge planning2,220 ÷ 6003.7 days
Bed days freed2,400 − 2,220 (or 0.3 × 600)180 bed days
Capacity freed per day180 ÷ 31about 5.8 beds

A 0.3-day drop looks small, but with the same 600 discharges it releases 180 bed days in the month: room for more admissions without adding beds, provided the shorter stays are clinically safe.

Indicative ALOS range in India

Treat ALOS benchmarks as indicative, not universal targets. Many secondary hospitals track overall ALOS in the 3 to 5 day range, while complex tertiary units may naturally sit higher. A maternity-focused nursing home, an oncology centre and an ICU-led tertiary hospital will have very different stay patterns, so compare ALOS by specialty, consultant, payer type and ward rather than relying on one hospital-wide number.

Common causes of high ALOS

  • Discharge planning that starts on the morning of discharge instead of at admission
  • Diagnostic reports needed for discharge arriving late in the day
  • Take-home medicines and final bills prepared only after clinical clearance
  • Cashless approvals and TPA queries not ready when the patient is fit to go
  • Late family counselling on discharge instructions or home care

Each of these may add only a few hours, but together they can push a 2 pm discharge past midnight and add a whole inpatient day.

Frequently asked questions

What is the ALOS full form in hospital?

ALOS stands for Average Length of Stay. In a hospital it is the average number of inpatient days used for each discharge in a selected period.

What is the ALOS formula?

ALOS = Total inpatient days ÷ Total discharges (including deaths), with both numbers taken for the same period.

How to calculate ALOS?

Add up inpatient days for the period (usually the sum of the daily midnight census), count all discharges including deaths in the same period, and divide the first by the second. For example, 2,400 inpatient days and 600 discharges give an ALOS of 4.0 days.

Should deaths be included in the ALOS calculation formula?

Yes. The standard ALOS formula divides by total discharges including deaths, because a patient who dies in hospital has also left the bed and used inpatient days.

What is a good ALOS for an Indian hospital?

There is no universal target. As an indicative range, many secondary hospitals track overall ALOS of 3 to 5 days, while complex tertiary units may naturally sit higher. Compare ALOS by specialty and ward rather than as one number.

Is lower ALOS always better?

No. Lower ALOS is useful only when readmission risk, clinical outcomes and patient safety are protected. The goal is to remove avoidable administrative days, not necessary care.
