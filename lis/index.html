LIS Full Form: What is a Laboratory Information System?

LIS full form in medical is Laboratory Information System. An LIS is software that runs a clinical or pathology laboratory: test orders, sample collection and tracking, results, approvals, report delivery and billing. Its close relative, a LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System), is more common in pharma and research labs.

LIS full form

What LIS and LIMS stand for.

The abbreviations have a few meanings. In medical laboratory use, LIS means the first.

Laboratory Information System (LIS)

The medical laboratory meaning. Software that manages patient samples, tests, results and reports in hospital labs, pathology labs and diagnostic centres.

Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)

Software built around samples and batches, common in pharmaceutical, research and quality-control labs. Many Indian vendors use LIMS and LIS for the same pathology software.

Lateral internal sphincterotomy (LIS)

A surgical meaning: an operation used to treat chronic anal fissure. Unrelated to laboratory software.

What is an LIS

What a laboratory information system does.

An LIS follows every test from the order to the bill. These are its core functions.

Registration and orders

Register patients and receive test orders from doctors, the hospital HMIS, walk-ins and referral partners.

Sample collection

Record centre and home collection, label each sample and link it to the patient and order.

Sample tracking

Follow each sample from collection to the lab bench, so delayed or missing samples are visible.

Results

Enter results or receive them from connected analyzers, with reference ranges and flags.

Validation and approval

Pathologist review, approval and sign-off before a report is released.

Report delivery

Release reports to patients, referring doctors and the hospital record.

Billing

Charge every test correctly for cash, corporate, insurance and B2B accounts.

Referrals and B2B

Manage referring doctors, collection centres and partner labs, with their volumes and statements.

Turnaround and analytics

Lab TAT, pending tests, test volumes and revenue by test, department and referrer.

LIS workflow

How a test moves through the LIS.

Order

A doctor, the front desk or a partner creates a test order for the patient.

Collect

The sample is collected at the centre, ward or patient's home and labelled against the order.

Process

The sample reaches the lab, is run on the analyzer or bench, and results are captured.

Approve

A pathologist validates and approves the results.

Deliver and bill

The report goes to the patient, doctor and hospital record, and the charge goes to the bill.

How an LIS connects to the hospital system.

In a hospital, the LIS works with the HMIS and, for imaging, the RIS and PACS.

RIS

Does for radiology what the LIS does for the lab. What is a RIS?

PACS

Stores and displays radiology images. What is PACS?

Where lab revenue leaks

A test that is processed but never billed, a sample collected but never run, or a discount without approval is lost revenue. When the LIS and billing share one record, each test is accounted for.

Frequently asked questions

Laboratory information system: common questions

What is LIS full form in medical?

In medical and laboratory use, LIS stands for Laboratory Information System: software that manages a clinical laboratory's samples, tests, results, approvals and reports. In surgery, LIS can also mean lateral internal sphincterotomy, an operation for anal fissure, which is unrelated to lab software.

What is a laboratory information system?

A laboratory information system (LIS) is software that runs a clinical or pathology laboratory: patient and sample registration, test orders, sample collection and tracking, result entry or capture from analyzers, validation and approval, report delivery and billing.

What is the full form of LIMS?

LIMS stands for Laboratory Information Management System. The term is common in pharmaceutical, research and industrial labs, where the focus is on samples, batches and quality testing. Clinical labs and hospitals more often call their system an LIS, though many vendors use the two terms interchangeably.

What is the difference between LIS and LIMS?

An LIS is built around the patient: orders from doctors, patient samples, clinical results and reports. A LIMS is built around samples and batches, often for pharma, research or quality-control labs. In Indian hospitals and diagnostic centres the two names are often used for the same pathology lab software.

What is pathology lab software?

Pathology lab software is the LIS used by a pathology or diagnostic laboratory. It manages bookings, home and centre sample collection, sample tracking, results, doctor approval, report delivery, billing and referral or B2B accounts.

How does an LIS work with an HMIS?

The HMIS sends lab orders from OPD, IPD and emergency to the LIS with the patient's details. The LIS sends back approved results to the patient record and the test charge to the bill, so no test is re-typed and no test performed goes unbilled.

Can an LIS connect to lab analyzers?

Yes, most LIS products can receive results directly from analyzers so technicians do not type them in. With Shucon, analyzer integration is planned during onboarding based on the equipment the lab uses.
