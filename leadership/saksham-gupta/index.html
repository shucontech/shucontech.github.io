About Saksham

Production AI engineering for healthcare's operational core.

Saksham founded Shucon MedAI to help healthcare organizations move beyond isolated AI demonstrations and deploy intelligent systems inside the workflows that run care, revenue and operations.

Leadership principles

Build around real work, not AI theatre.

Saksham's approach to Shucon's product and customer engagements is grounded in operational outcomes and clear controls.

01

Start with the workflow

Understand the people, systems, decisions and exceptions before selecting the automation approach.

02

Connect the context

Give agents the clinical, operational and financial context required to make useful decisions.

03

Govern consequential actions

Build permissions, auditability, approvals and failure handling into the operating model.

04

Measure production outcomes

Evaluate coverage, accuracy, turnaround time and business impact rather than demonstration quality.
