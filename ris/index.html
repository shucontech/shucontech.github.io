Radiology software explained

What is a Radiology Information System (RIS)?

A radiology information system (RIS) is software that manages the workflow of a radiology department or imaging centre. It handles patient scheduling, imaging orders, modality worklists, study tracking, radiologist reporting, report delivery and billing. The images themselves are stored in a PACS.

What is RIS

What a radiology information system does.

RIS covers everything in radiology except storing and viewing the images. These are its core functions.

Registration and scheduling

Book patients by modality, room and slot, manage walk-ins and send preparation instructions and reminders.

Orders

Receive imaging orders from OPD, IPD, emergency and referring doctors with the clinical indication attached.

Modality worklists

Send each scanner the list of scheduled exams so technologists select the patient instead of typing details.

Study tracking

Follow every study from arrival to scan, reporting, verification and delivery, and see what is pending.

Radiologist reporting

Report with templates, assign studies to radiologists, verify and sign reports.

Report delivery

Release finalised reports to patients, referring doctors and the hospital record.

Billing

Attach the correct charge to every study for cash, corporate, insurance and referral accounts.

Referrals

Track referring doctors and B2B accounts, their volumes and their reports.

Analytics

Turnaround time, modality utilisation, pending reports and revenue by modality and referrer.

RIS workflow

How a study moves through the RIS.

Order

A doctor or the front desk creates an imaging order for the patient.

Schedule

The RIS books the modality and slot and the patient is registered on arrival.

Scan

The study appears on the scanner worklist; images are sent to the PACS with the matching order.

Report

The study is assigned to a radiologist, reported, verified and signed.

Deliver and bill

The report goes to the patient, the referring doctor and the hospital record, and the charge goes to the bill.

RIS vs PACS

RIS manages the work. PACS manages the images.

Radiology needs both. The RIS tells the scanner what to do; the PACS keeps what the scanner produces.

RIS

Radiology information system. Scheduling, worklists, study status, reporting, report delivery and billing.

PACS

Picture archiving and communication system. Stores, routes and displays images in the DICOM format. What is PACS?

HMIS

Hospital management information system. Sends radiology orders and receives reports and charges on the patient record and bill. What is HMIS?

LIS

Laboratory information system. Does for the lab what the RIS does for radiology: samples, results, approvals and reports.

Where radiology revenue leaks

A study that is scanned but never reported, delivered or billed is lost revenue. When the RIS, PACS and billing share one record, every performed study is accounted for.

Frequently asked questions

Radiology information system: common questions

What is a radiology information system?

A radiology information system (RIS) is software that manages the workflow of a radiology department or imaging centre: patient registration and scheduling, imaging orders, modality worklists, study tracking, radiologist reporting, report delivery and billing.

What is RIS in radiology?

In radiology, RIS stands for radiology information system. It manages the patient and workflow side of imaging, while the PACS manages the images themselves.

What is the difference between RIS and PACS?

RIS manages the work: appointments, worklists, study status, reports and billing. PACS (picture archiving and communication system) stores and displays the images. The RIS tells the scanner which patient and exam to perform; the PACS stores what the scanner produces.

What is a modality worklist in RIS?

A modality worklist is the list of scheduled exams that the RIS sends to each scanner, such as CT or MRI. The technologist picks the patient from the list instead of typing details, so images reach the PACS with the correct patient and order.

How does RIS work with HMIS?

The HMIS sends radiology orders from OPD, IPD and emergency to the RIS. The RIS returns the study status, the finalised report and the charge, so the report appears in the patient record and the study appears on the bill.

Who uses a radiology information system?

Front-desk and scheduling staff, radiographers and technologists, radiologists, billing teams and centre managers in hospital radiology departments, diagnostic centres and teleradiology providers.

What are the benefits of a radiology information system?

Fewer manual entry errors, shorter waiting times through better scheduling, faster report turnaround, reports delivered to patients and referring doctors on time, and every performed study captured for billing.

What is radiology reporting software?

Radiology reporting software is the part of a RIS that radiologists use to write, verify and sign reports, usually with templates, study assignment and turnaround tracking. In Shucon it is part of the RIS and linked to the PACS and billing.
