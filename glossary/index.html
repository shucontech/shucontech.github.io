Hospital departments

Hospital departments: full forms and meanings.

OPD full form: Outpatient Department

The department where patients are seen without admission: consultations, minor procedures, prescriptions and investigations, after which the patient goes home.

IPD full form: Inpatient Department

Care for patients admitted to the hospital and occupying a bed, from admission to discharge.

OT full form: Operation Theatre

The sterile area where surgical procedures are performed. Hospitals often track OT utilisation and OT billing separately.

ICU full form: Intensive Care Unit

A unit for critically ill patients needing close monitoring and organ support.

MRD full form: Medical Records Department

The department that maintains, stores and retrieves patient medical records and handles record requests.

CSSD full form: Central Sterile Services Department

The department that cleans, sterilises and supplies surgical instruments and sterile items to the OT and wards.

TPA full form: Third Party Administrator

An intermediary licensed to process health insurance claims on behalf of insurers. The hospital's TPA or insurance desk handles pre-authorisation, queries and claim settlement. Learn more

MIS full form: Management Information System

In a hospital, the set of management reports on revenue, occupancy, collections, claims and departmental performance.

Hospital software and standards

Hospital software and standards: full forms and meanings.

HMIS full form: Hospital Management Information System

Software that runs a hospital's clinical, administrative and financial work. In public health, HMIS also means Health Management Information System. Learn more

HMS full form: Hospital Management System

Another name for hospital management software, usually treated as a synonym of HMIS. Learn more

HIS full form: Hospital Information System

A common name for the same category of hospital software, often used for larger hospitals. Learn more

EMR full form: Electronic Medical Record

The digital clinical record of a patient within one hospital or clinic: notes, diagnoses, orders, results and medicines.

EHR full form: Electronic Health Record

A patient's digital health record designed to be shared across providers, covering care from more than one facility.

LIS full form: Laboratory Information System

Software that runs a clinical or pathology laboratory: samples, results, approvals, reports and billing. Learn more

LIMS full form: Laboratory Information Management System

Laboratory software built around samples and batches, common in pharma and research labs; often used interchangeably with LIS. Learn more

RIS full form: Radiology Information System

Software that manages radiology scheduling, worklists, reporting, report delivery and billing. Learn more

PACS full form: Picture Archiving and Communication System

The system that stores, distributes and displays medical images such as X-ray, CT and MRI. Learn more

DICOM full form: Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine

The international standard format and network protocol for medical images, used by scanners and PACS. Learn more

HL7 full form: Health Level Seven

A family of standards for exchanging clinical and administrative messages between healthcare systems, such as orders and results.

FHIR full form: Fast Healthcare Interoperability Resources

An HL7 standard that exchanges health data through modern web APIs.

CPOE full form: Computerized Physician Order Entry

Doctors entering medication, lab and radiology orders directly into the hospital system instead of on paper.

CDSS full form: Clinical Decision Support System

Software that gives clinicians alerts, reminders or recommendations based on patient data, such as drug interaction warnings.

RCM full form: Revenue Cycle Management

The process of capturing, billing and collecting revenue for patient care, from registration and estimates to claim settlement. Learn more

Accreditation and government schemes

Accreditation and government schemes: full forms and meanings.

NABH full form: National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers

The Indian board, part of the Quality Council of India, that accredits hospitals and healthcare providers on quality and patient-safety standards.

NABL full form: National Accreditation Board for Testing and Calibration Laboratories

The Indian board that accredits testing laboratories, including medical laboratories, and calibration laboratories.

ABDM full form: Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission

India's national programme to build digital health infrastructure, including health IDs, facility and professional registries, and record sharing.

ABHA full form: Ayushman Bharat Health Account

A unique health ID for individuals under ABDM, used to link and share their digital health records.

PM-JAY full form: Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

The Ayushman Bharat government health insurance scheme covering hospitalisation for eligible families at empanelled hospitals.

CGHS full form: Central Government Health Scheme

The health scheme for central government employees, pensioners and their dependants, using CGHS wellness centres and empanelled hospitals.

ECHS full form: Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme

The health scheme for ex-servicemen and their dependants, using ECHS polyclinics and empanelled hospitals.

NHCX full form: National Health Claims Exchange

A digital platform for exchanging health insurance claim information between hospitals, insurers and TPAs in a standard format.

Insurance and claims

Insurance and claims: full forms and meanings.

GIPSA full form: General Insurers' (Public Sector) Association

The association of public-sector general insurers in India. GIPSA hospital networks use agreed package rates for cashless treatment.

PPN full form: Preferred Provider Network

A network of hospitals that have agreed rates with insurers for cashless treatment. Non-PPN hospitals are outside that agreement.

Pre-auth full form: Pre-authorisation

The insurer or TPA approval a hospital requests before cashless treatment, stating the expected cost.

Hospital metrics

Hospital metrics: full forms and meanings.

ALOS full form: Average Length of Stay

Total inpatient days divided by total discharges, including deaths, for the same period. Learn more

BOR full form: Bed Occupancy Rate

Occupied bed days divided by available bed days, multiplied by 100. Learn more

ARPOB full form: Average Revenue Per Occupied Bed

Net IPD revenue divided by occupied bed days for the same period. Learn more

ADC full form: Average Daily Census

Total inpatient service days divided by the number of days in the period: the average number of inpatients per day. Learn more

BTR full form: Bed Turnover Rate

Total discharges divided by average available beds: how many patients each bed served in the period. Learn more

CMI full form: Case Mix Index

The average relative weight of the cases a hospital treats, used to compare patient complexity across periods or hospitals.

TAT full form: Turnaround Time

The time taken to complete a process, such as a lab report, discharge or claim approval.

Pharmacy and inventory

Pharmacy and inventory: full forms and meanings.

GRN full form: Goods Receipt Note

The record created when purchased goods are received and checked against the purchase order and invoice.

FEFO full form: First Expiry, First Out

Issuing the stock that expires first, regardless of when it arrived. Standard for medicines.

FIFO full form: First In, First Out

Issuing the oldest stock received first.

VED full form: Vital, Essential, Desirable

Classifying hospital items by how critical they are to patient care, often combined with ABC analysis by value.

ABC analysis full form: Always, Better, Control

Classifying inventory by annual consumption value: A items are the few high-value items, C items the many low-value ones.

Frequently asked questions

Hospital full forms: common questions

What is the full form of OPD and IPD in a hospital?

OPD stands for Outpatient Department, where patients are seen without admission. IPD stands for Inpatient Department, for patients admitted and occupying a bed.

What is TPA full form in hospital?

TPA stands for Third Party Administrator, an intermediary that processes health insurance claims for insurers. The hospital's TPA desk handles pre-authorisation, queries and claim settlement.

What is MRD full form in hospital?

MRD stands for Medical Records Department, which maintains, stores and retrieves patient medical records.

What is CSSD full form in medical?

CSSD stands for Central Sterile Services Department, which cleans, sterilises and supplies surgical instruments and sterile items.

What is NABH full form?

NABH stands for National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers, the Indian board that accredits hospitals on quality and patient-safety standards.

What is the full form of EMR and EHR?

EMR stands for Electronic Medical Record, the digital clinical record within one hospital. EHR stands for Electronic Health Record, a record designed to be shared across providers.

What is ABHA and ABDM full form?

ABHA stands for Ayushman Bharat Health Account, an individual health ID. ABDM stands for Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission, India's national digital health programme.

What is RCM full form in healthcare?

RCM stands for Revenue Cycle Management: capturing, billing and collecting revenue for patient care, from registration and estimates to claim settlement.
