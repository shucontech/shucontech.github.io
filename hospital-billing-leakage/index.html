Common patterns

Billing leakage is usually a broken connection, not a missing report.

Services delivered but not billed

A procedure, investigation, consumable or bedside service is recorded clinically but never reaches the patient bill.

Late cancellations

A bill is cancelled after medicine, stock or a service has already been delivered, without reversing the operational event.

Discount exceptions

A concession falls outside policy, lacks an approval trail or is repeatedly applied in an unusual pattern.

Refund mismatches

A refund is processed without matching the original payment, fulfilment status or inventory reversal.

Discharge omissions

Charges accumulated across wards, pharmacy, diagnostics and procedures are absent from the final IPD bill.

Claim discrepancies

The clinical record, submitted claim and hospital bill disagree, creating avoidable deductions or delayed resolution.

Practical reconciliation

Ask whether the complete transaction agrees.

Order vs delivery

Was the prescribed procedure, investigation or medicine actually delivered?

Delivery vs charge

Does every fulfilled event have the correct billable line at the correct tariff?

Charge vs approval

Are discounts, cancellations and refunds supported by policy and an auditable approval?

Bill vs payment

Do cash, online, insurer and outstanding balances reconcile to the final bill?
