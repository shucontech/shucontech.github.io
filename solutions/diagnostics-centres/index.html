Frequently asked questions

Diagnostic centre software: common questions

What does the diagnostics platform cover?

Bookings, home collection, sample tracking, laboratory workflows, radiology scheduling and reporting, report delivery, billing, referrals and B2B accounts, on one connected platform.

Does it work as a LIS and RIS?

Yes. It covers laboratory information system workflows for samples, results and approvals, and radiology information system workflows for scheduling, modality worklists and reporting.

How do AI agents help a diagnostic centre?

Agents flag delayed samples and reports, unbilled tests, referral and collection gaps, and patients who need follow-up calls, then route each item to the responsible team.

Can it support multiple branches and collection centres?

Yes. Multi-location chains can manage branches, collection centres and central processing labs from one platform with role-based access.

Can it connect to lab analyzers and PACS?

Integrations with analyzers, PACS and existing systems are planned during onboarding based on your equipment and workflows.
